# Sector Templates

## General

Use for ordinary operating companies when no clearer sector template is available.

Primary drivers:
- Revenue growth
- Gross margin
- Expense ratios

## Manufacturing

Use when volume, pricing, and utilization are important.

Primary drivers:
- Sales volume
- Average selling price
- Gross margin
- Capex and depreciation if the model is extended

## Consumer

Use when brand strength, channels, or store productivity matter.

Primary drivers:
- Same-store sales or volume growth
- Price/mix
- Gross margin
- Selling expense ratio

## Software or internet

Use only for simple top-line and margin scaffolds.

Primary drivers:
- Revenue growth
- Gross margin
- R&D ratio
- Sales efficiency assumptions

Do not use this scaffold alone for complex platform economics if the user needs detailed cohort logic.
